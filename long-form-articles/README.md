## Readme for long-form articles

This folder contains samples of long-form business and technology articles I developed for publication. I conducted interviews with C-level executives, transcribed the interviews, and used the information gathered to develop structured, reader-focused articles.

The featured sample is a magazine cover story about Acer India, published in CIOReview India's August 2018 Agriculture Special issue.

**Tools and technologies**

- Microsoft Word: I used word-processing tools to draft and edit article content, where applicable.
- PDF: I use PDF files to present published articles for portfolio review.

**Writing and editorial skills**

The articles demonstrate my experience in the following areas:

- Executive interviews: I conducted interviews with C-level executives to gather insights and information.
- Interview transcription: I transcribed interviews to capture executive perspectives and key information.
- Long-form writing: I developed detailed articles from interview transcripts and source material.
- Business and technology writing: I wrote about business initiatives, technology solutions, products, and services.
- Company profiling: I presented company capabilities, offerings, and market positioning.
- Content organization: I organized information into cohesive narratives using headings, subheadings, and thematic sections.
- Editing and proofreading: I edited and proofread content to improve clarity, readability, consistency, and flow.
